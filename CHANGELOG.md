# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

First bowl served.

- Alphabet soup with real physics: letters float, bump and drift, and you can only spell through letters that touch.
- Stir with your finger, mouse or the arrow keys to herd letters together.
- Daily Bowl: the same 50 letters for everyone, no clock, with ratings, streaks, a 7-day history and a spoiler-free result to copy.
- Rush: 90 seconds with a combo multiplier. Zen: endless soup.
- Golden noodles, chili noodles, star pasta and one stubborn meatball.
- A robot chef who slurps your words and blows a kiss for the long ones.
- Records and nine achievements.
- Drag, tap or keyboard play. Bowls in progress survive a reload.
