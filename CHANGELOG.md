# Changelog

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-26)

- When a bowl ends, the result card shows the best word still hiding in the leftovers, so you know what got away. It picks an everyday word when there is one nearly as long, and says "Longest word left in the bowl" when it can only offer a dictionary oddity.
- On a phone held sideways, the Daily result card sits in two columns and fits without scrolling.
- The shared result now ends with the full link, https://games.oddvale.com/soupspell/.
- Hints point at everyday words now (HONK, not NOH) when the bowl has one.
- Copy result never leaves you stuck: if your browser blocks copying, the result appears in the card, selected, ready to copy by hand.
- Bigger noodles on phones: the bowl gets more of the screen (about 37px noodles in full screen on a small phone), and the word box no longer grows when you start tracing, so letters stay put under your finger.
- Every button is at least 44px, also on a phone held sideways.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-26)

Plays better on phones:
- Full screen on every phone, iPhone included: tap "Play full screen" under the bowl. The bowl and buttons re-fit to the whole screen, upright or sideways, and stay clear of the notch.
- A backspace button next to Clear takes off just the last letter.
- While you trace a word with your finger, the letter under it pops up in a bubble above, so your finger never hides it.
- Bigger buttons (Clear, Stuck?, I'm full), larger hint and message text, and a little buzz when you pick a letter, slurp a word or miss (Android, when sound is on).

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-25)

- Tell us how hard today's Daily Bowl was with one tap, and see how your score did against everyone else's today. Anonymous: nothing about you is stored.
- A new "Tell us" card under the game: thumbs up or down and a few one-tap notes, no account needed.
- A Privacy link in the footer.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

First bowl served.

- Alphabet soup with real physics: letters float, bump and drift, and you can only spell through letters that touch.
- Stir with your finger, mouse or the arrow keys to herd letters together.
- Daily Bowl: the same 50 letters for everyone, no clock, with ratings, streaks, a 7-day history and a spoiler-free result to copy.
- Rush: 90 seconds with a combo multiplier. Zen: endless soup.
- Golden noodles, chili noodles, star pasta and one stubborn meatball.
- A robot chef who slurps your words and blows a kiss for the long ones.
- Records and nine achievements.
- Drag, tap or keyboard play. Bowls in progress survive a reload.
